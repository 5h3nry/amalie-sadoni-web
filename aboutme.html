About Me

It all began in Bergen, a city on the west coast of Norway. This is where I started my my life and first became a Yoga Teacher teaching mindful yoga classes for a government ran scheme. Whilst the first couple of sessions had only my friends show up, it quickly became incredibly popular, causing the government to replicate our classes across the country!
